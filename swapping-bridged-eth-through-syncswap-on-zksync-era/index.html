Swapping bridged ETH through SyncSwap on zkSync Era

To swap bridged ETH on zkSync Era, connect MetaMask, keep ETH for gas, choose your token, review the pool quote and minimum received, then sign and confirm.

By Crypto Readout EditorialNov 23, 2026 · 03:33 UTC3 min read#b16f50

To swap bridged ETH for another token on zkSync Era, connect the wallet holding that ETH to SyncSwap, select ETH and your destination token, review the quote, and sign the transaction. The swap trades against liquidity pools, while ETH pays the network gas needed to submit it, so keep some ETH aside for that cost.

Bridged ETH must already be on Era in the wallet you connect; ETH held on Ethereum is a separate balance. For the steps that get ETH onto Era and ready for a trade, see how to fund SyncSwap after bridging. SyncSwap uses liquidity pools to make the trade, so the amount of your destination token depends on the quote when you submit.

How do you swap bridged ETH for a token on zkSync Era?

A first swap starts with the wallet and network, then moves through the token selection, quote review, and transaction confirmation.

  1. Connect the wallet on Era. Open SyncSwap and connect MetaMask, or another wallet supported by the app. Select zkSync Era in the wallet and confirm that the connected wallet is the one that received the bridged ETH. A wallet can show different balances on different networks.
  2. Leave ETH for gas. Look at the wallet’s ETH balance on Era before setting the trade amount. Keep a portion available for gas: if you swap the full balance, you may not have enough left to submit the transaction. The amount needed can vary with the transaction, so leave a reserve rather than treating the whole balance as spendable.
  3. Choose the tokens and amount. Set ETH as the token you are paying and choose the token you want to receive. Enter the amount of ETH to swap. The app uses the available liquidity pools to calculate a quote for that trade.
  4. Review the quote before signing. Check the destination token, quoted amount, fee, and minimum received shown for the trade. The minimum received is the lowest amount the transaction will accept under its settings. If the quote or minimum is lower than you are willing to accept, adjust the amount or review the trade again before proceeding.
  5. Submit and confirm. Start the swap and read the wallet prompt, including the network and transaction details. Approve the prompt to sign and submit it. Wait for the transaction to confirm on Era, then check the transaction result and the destination token balance in your wallet.

Take a reader whose bridged ETH is already visible in MetaMask on Era and who has chosen a destination token. They connect that wallet, keep some ETH unspent for gas, and enter the amount they intend to trade. The app returns a pool quote and minimum received; after reviewing those details, the reader signs the transaction. Once it confirms, the token balance should reflect the swap outcome. The task is complete when the wallet is still on Era, the transaction is confirmed, and the resulting balance is visible.

Related

Do I need to bridge ETH to zkSync Era before swapping?

Yes. The ETH must be on zkSync Era in the connected wallet; ETH on Ethereum is a separate balance and cannot be spent in an Era swap.

Which wallet can I use with SyncSwap?

Use MetaMask or another wallet supported by the app, connected to zkSync Era and holding the bridged ETH you plan to swap.
