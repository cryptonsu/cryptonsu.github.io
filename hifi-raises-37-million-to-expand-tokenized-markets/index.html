HIFI raises $37 million to expand tokenized markets

HIFI raised $37 million in a Series A led by Left Lane Capital, funding expansion from stablecoin settlement into cards and tokenized capital markets for institutions.

Crypto Record NewsroomSep 24, 20262 min read

HIFI raised $37 million in a Series A led by Left Lane Capital, giving the company capital to expand from stablecoin settlement into cards and tokenized capital markets. In its funding announcement on September 24, HIFI said it would use the money to pursue additional regulatory licenses, grow its team and extend its products.

The system HIFI describes connects money movement, compliance and settlement through one API integration across bank rails and digital assets, according to The Block’s report on the round. A business connects to the API; HIFI’s infrastructure links its payment or asset workflow to the relevant rails; then the transaction settles. For a tokenized repo trade HIFI supported, the cash leg was USDCx, and settlement took place on the Canton Network.

How does HIFI connect stablecoin payments to tokenized assets?

HIFI’s API brings money movement, compliance and settlement into the same integration, The Block reported. That means a company can use the platform to connect bank rails with digital assets without building separate integrations for each part of the workflow. The announcement describes the larger change as moving money and assets into programmable ledger entries that can interact directly, rather than requiring institutions to exchange messages and reconcile records afterward.

HIFI said it processes more than $7 billion a year. It also reported that customers using its platform had onboarded more than 10,000 businesses and 200,000 individuals. In July, HIFI’s platform took part in a DTCC pilot for tokenized repo, the company said. It also supported a live repo trade on Tradeweb between DRW and Marex. That transaction settled on Canton, with USDCx serving as the cash leg.

What will HIFI spend the Series A on?

The company said the funding will support licensing, hiring and product expansion. HIFI plans to add licenses so more of its infrastructure operates under the company directly. It also plans to expand its products from payments into cards and capital markets, where it identified custody and control as unresolved issues. Matt Miller of Left Lane Capital will join HIFI’s board as part of the round, according to the announcement.

HIFI said it had also launched stablecoin-funded payouts through Visa Direct and integrated with Circle Payments Network. The funding therefore backs an expansion from payment settlement toward financial products involving tokenized assets. Its repo work shows how that infrastructure can connect a tokenized asset workflow with a stablecoin cash leg; the Series A will fund HIFI’s effort to extend that model into cards and capital markets.
