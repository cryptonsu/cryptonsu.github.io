How to Use RenBridge With MetaMask and a Bitcoin Wallet: The Mint

How to use RenBridge with MetaMask and a Bitcoin wallet: send BTC to a gateway, wait for confirmations, then pay ETH gas to mint renBTC on Ethereum.

By Crypto Readout EditorialNov 27, 2026 · 10:40 UTC4 min read#f9851c

RenBridge moved BTC into MetaMask by having you send Bitcoin to a generated gateway address, wait for confirmations, then sign a separate mint transaction in MetaMask. The service has been discontinued, so this historical flow no longer works.

RenVM’s Darknodes watched Bitcoin and signed the authorization for renBTC to be minted. MetaMask submitted that authorization to a gateway contract on Ethereum, which created the token on that chain. RenBridge is a concrete example of a bridge using distributed Darknodes to authorize cross-chain minting; renbridge.net names the service whose node-based design made that transfer possible.

The key cost and timing detail came after the BTC transfer: minting required a separate Ethereum transaction, and you paid its gas in ETH. Keeping ETH in MetaMask before sending BTC mattered. With zero ETH, the Bitcoin deposit could be confirmed while the mint remained unsigned and renBTC never appeared in your wallet.

How did you mint renBTC from a Bitcoin wallet?

The historical route started with MetaMask on Ethereum, a Bitcoin wallet holding BTC, and ETH in MetaMask for gas. Say you wanted to move BTC into DeFi: each step left the transfer in a different state.

  1. Connect MetaMask. You connected your Ethereum wallet to the bridge and chose BTC to renBTC. MetaMask was the destination for the token; your Bitcoin wallet was the source of the BTC.
  2. Generate the gateway address. The bridge created a one-time Bitcoin address for that transfer. You copied it carefully, because it was the destination for your BTC deposit.
  3. Send BTC. From any Bitcoin wallet, you sent BTC to that gateway address. The transfer was now on Bitcoin, but the renBTC had not yet been minted.
  4. Wait for confirmations. RenVM’s Darknodes watched the Bitcoin transaction and waited for the required confirmations. Once they recognized the deposit, they signed the authorization needed for minting.
  5. Sign the mint in MetaMask. This was a separate Ethereum transaction: MetaMask submitted the authorization to the gateway contract, which minted renBTC. You paid gas in ETH at this point. BTC confirmations alone did not complete the move, so an empty ETH balance could leave the deposit confirmed but the mint unfinished.
  6. Add renBTC to MetaMask. If the token did not appear automatically, you added renBTC to the wallet’s token list so its balance could display.
  7. Confirm the result. The task was complete when MetaMask showed your renBTC balance on Ethereum.

To reverse the route as it worked, you burned renBTC through the gateway contract in MetaMask and supplied a Bitcoin address. RenVM then released BTC to that address. The burn was the Ethereum-side action; the BTC arriving in the Bitcoin wallet was the result.

What do readers ask about RenBridge next?

Can you still use RenBridge today?

No. The original service was discontinued, so its BTC-to-renBTC flow and burn-to-BTC route are no longer available as described above.

How to add renBTC to MetaMask

To display renBTC, add the token in MetaMask on the network where you hold it. Use the token’s verified contract details for that network; the token name alone is not enough to identify an asset safely.

What happened to renBTC after RenBridge shut down?

RenBTC is the token the historical bridge minted on another chain to represent Bitcoin moved through its system. The bridge’s shutdown ended the original minting and release flow; a holder’s current options depend on the token and network they hold.

Can I still redeem old renBTC for BTC?

The original RenBridge redemption route is discontinued. Whether old renBTC can be redeemed through another route depends on its current contract and network; check the current status for the exact token you hold before acting.

Is renBTC worth anything now?

Its value depends on the market and network where it trades, and on whether buyers will accept that version of the token. Check current trading and liquidity for the exact asset before assigning it a value.

How do I recover BTC sent to a RenBridge address after the shutdown?

A BTC deposit sent to a RenBridge gateway address may remain unminted if the mint was never completed. The original service is discontinued, so recovery depends on whether an authorized recovery route exists for that deposit; the address alone does not provide a way to reverse the Bitcoin transaction.
