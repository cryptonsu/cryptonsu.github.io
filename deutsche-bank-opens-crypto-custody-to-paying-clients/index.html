Deutsche Bank opens crypto custody to paying clients

Deutsche Bank has opened crypto custody to paying clients and signed a pact with Deutsche Asset Management to explore tokenized funds, ETCs and stablecoins.

By Crypto Readout EditorialNov 12, 2026 · 01:15 UTC2 min read#aea1d4

Deutsche Bank has opened its digital asset custody service to paying clients and signed a memorandum with Deutsche Asset Management to explore digital asset products, bringing the bank’s custody operations into service. The Reuters report published by Euronext says the service went live on November 11.

What does the custody service let clients do?

It lets corporate and institutional customers hold selected digital assets with Deutsche Bank, which manages the wallets and private keys and can transfer assets to other parties, according to The Block’s report on the launch. A private key is the secret credential that authorizes control of crypto in a wallet; the custody service puts its management with the bank.

The Block reports that the system uses both warm and cold storage, hardware-based protection for private keys, and transaction approvals involving multiple people. Warm storage keeps keys accessible to connected systems for transactions; cold storage keeps them offline. Together, these are parts of the bank’s process for holding assets and authorizing transfers.

The service was announced in September for clients in Europe. At launch, supported assets include bitcoin, ether and stablecoins USDC, EURC and EURAU, The Block reported. Deutsche Bank partnered with Taurus in 2023 to develop custody and tokenization services, according to the same report.

What does the agreement with Deutsche Asset Management cover?

The memorandum of understanding asks the two firms to explore tokenized money market funds, crypto-asset exchange-traded commodities, and stablecoins. The Reuters report describes the agreement as an exploration of commercial and technical collaboration; it does not announce a product launch.

The Block says the firms will also examine custody and settlement, liquidity management, treasury and distribution using Deutsche Bank’s custody platform. Deutsche Asset Management’s chief product officer, Manfred Bauer, said the firm was planning to launch a tokenized money market fund. The agreement connects custody infrastructure with possible products and the services needed to manage and distribute them.

What has Deutsche Bank committed to next?

Paul Maley, Deutsche Bank’s global head of trust and securities services, said the bank aims over the medium term to build an integrated, regulated system for custody, tokenization and settlement, Reuters reported. The launch puts custody into operation; the wider system remains an aim. The memorandum sets out areas to explore, not a confirmed schedule for new products.
