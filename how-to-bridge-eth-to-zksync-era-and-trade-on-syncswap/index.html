How to bridge ETH to zkSync Era and trade on SyncSwap

Bridge ETH from Ethereum to zkSync Era through the canonical bridge or a faster third-party route, verify the balance, then connect MetaMask to SyncSwap.

By Crypto Readout EditorialNov 19, 2026 · 18:24 UTC4 min read#567c8c

Start with ETH on Ethereum mainnet in MetaMask, because a bridge deposit needs L1 ETH to cover the transfer fee. The bridge sends ETH to your address on zkSync Era; once the network processes it, that ETH can pay for gas and trades there.

What to have ready

Have ETH in MetaMask on Ethereum mainnet, with enough left over for the bridge fee. Keep the official bridge URL for the route you choose, and check the address before connecting or signing. An externally owned account (EOA) keeps the same address on Ethereum and zkSync Era, so the deposit can credit that address on the destination network.

Choose the canonical zkSync bridge when you want the direct contract route. Its L1 contract locks the deposited ETH and sends a priority operation to zkSync Era, where the request is processed and the ETH is credited. A third-party bridge such as Across or Orbiter may offer a faster fill using liquidity on the destination side; compare the quoted amount, fee, and route before signing. The ZKsync documentation on bridging assets describes how the canonical L1 and L2 contracts pass deposits between networks.

A canonical deposit quote has a moving part beyond Ethereum gas: it includes prepaid execution cost for the L2 request. That cost reflects zkSync Era’s processing and data-publication requirements, whose pricing is linked to L1 gas as well as L2 gas conditions. So a quote can change when either network’s gas prices move. Review the full quote immediately before signing; the ZKsync fee structure explains how L1 gas prices affect L2 transaction costs.

Bridge ETH and connect your wallet

Follow these steps to move ETH from Ethereum to zkSync Era and make it available for a trade.

  1. Open the official bridge you chose and connect MetaMask on Ethereum mainnet. The bridge should show your wallet address and Ethereum as the source network.
  2. Set Ethereum as the source, zkSync Era as the destination, and ETH as the asset. Enter the amount, then review the estimated fee and amount to arrive. The screen should show the route and destination before you sign.
  3. Sign the deposit in MetaMask and wait for the source transaction to confirm. For the canonical route, the request enters Ethereum’s L1 priority queue; zkSync Era credits ETH after it processes that request. Think of the queue as a line of authorized deliveries: the L1 transaction starts the transfer, and L2 processing completes it.
  4. Add zkSync Era Mainnet to MetaMask if it is not listed, using the official network details: chain ID 324 and ETH as the currency symbol. Switch to that network. MetaMask should now show your zkSync Era account, which uses the same EOA address as on Ethereum.
  5. Look up your address or deposit transaction on the zkSync Era block explorer. Confirm that the ETH credit appears there, then leave some ETH unswapped for gas on later approvals and swaps. ETH is the network’s gas token, so spending the entire balance on a trade can leave no ETH for the next transaction.
  6. With zkSync Era selected in MetaMask, open SyncSwap and connect the wallet. The connected interface should display your zkSync Era balance; that visible balance and wallet connection mean you are ready to trade.

Say you bridge part of your Ethereum ETH through the canonical route. MetaMask first shows the L1 deposit confirmation; after the request is processed, the explorer shows the credited ETH at your address. You switch networks, keep some ETH for gas, and connect to SyncSwap as the next step because it lets you use that zkSync Era balance to trade or access liquidity pools.

Related

How long does a deposit to zkSync Era take?

A canonical deposit takes the time needed for Ethereum confirmation and zkSync Era to process the queued request; the exact wait can vary with network activity. A third-party route may fill sooner from destination liquidity, but its quoted timing depends on that route. Use the explorer to confirm credit rather than relying only on the wallet’s network switch.

How do I withdraw from zkSync Era back to Ethereum?

Start a withdrawal on zkSync Era, then wait for the L2-to-L1 message and finalize the withdrawal on Ethereum. ZKsync documents a three-hour minimum delay for withdrawals; actual finalization can take longer depending on network activity and proof processing.

Before starting, make sure each prerequisite is in place:

  • Ethereum mainnet ETH in MetaMask: the L1 balance covers the deposit amount and fee.
  • L1 ETH reserved for the bridge fee: the quote shows a fee you can afford.
  • Official bridge URL: the connected page shows the intended source and zkSync Era destination.
