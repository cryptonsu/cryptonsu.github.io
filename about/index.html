About Crypto Record

Crypto Record reports on the crypto industry as a whole: blockchain networks and their upgrades, exchanges and custody, stablecoins and payments, decentralized finance, security incidents and the rules governments write for all of it. Each story sets out how the system in question works before it reports what changed.

We are independent and not affiliated with any project, token, exchange or fund we cover. Stories are built from primary sources such as filings, code repositories, on-chain records and official statements, and we correct errors openly in the story where they occurred. Nothing published here is financial advice.
