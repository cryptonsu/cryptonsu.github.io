About Crypto Readout

Crypto Readout reports news from across the crypto industry: Bitcoin and Ethereum, layer-2 networks, DeFi protocols, stablecoins, exchanges, security incidents and the regulation that shapes them. Each story walks through how the thing in the headline actually works, from the transaction flow to the rule change.

We are independent and not affiliated with any project, token, exchange or investor. Stories are built on primary sources such as filings, code repositories, governance forums and on-chain records, and they carry a dated correction note when we get something wrong. Crypto Readout is an independent news outlet, and nothing published here is financial advice.
